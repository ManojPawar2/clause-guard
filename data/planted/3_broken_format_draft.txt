Proposal for a Govt. Resolution regarding sanction of additional hostel stipend for students of professional courses.

It has been decided to sanction an additional hostel stipend of Rs. 2,000 per month to eligible students of professional courses admitted through the centralized admission process.

The stipend will be paid through the Maha-DBT portal.

It also covers handicapped students.

The concerned Directorates should give wide publicity to this decision.

This will come into effect from the academic year 2025-26.
