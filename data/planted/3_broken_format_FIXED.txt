Sanction of additional hostel stipend for students of professional courses.

GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA
Higher and Technical Education Department
Government Resolution No.: HST-2025/C.R.42/TE-4
Mantralaya, Mumbai - 400 032
Date: 15 May, 2025

Read :-
1) Government Circular, Higher and Technical Education Department, Unique Code 202407191857335408, dated 19.07.2024.

Preamble :-
The question of providing additional hostel assistance to students of professional courses admitted through the centralized admission process was under consideration of the Government.

Government Resolution :-
1. An additional hostel stipend of Rs. 2,000 per month is hereby sanctioned for eligible students of professional courses admitted through the centralized admission process.

2. The stipend shall be disbursed directly through the MahaDBT portal to the Aadhaar-linked bank accounts of eligible students.

3. The concerned Directorates shall give wide publicity to this decision through all media.

4. This Government Resolution shall come into effect from the academic year 2025-26.

This Government Resolution is available on the www.maharashtra.gov.in website with Unique Code 202505151230457808.

By order and in the name of the Governor of Maharashtra,

( K. K. Gajbhiye )
Under Secretary, Government of Maharashtra
